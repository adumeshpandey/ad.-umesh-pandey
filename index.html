About

Adv. Umesh Chandra Pandey is a dedicated senior lawyer with 20 years of experience representing clients in civil matters before the Civil Court, Sultanpur.

With an LL.B. qualification and a strong grounding in civil law, he approaches every matter with careful preparation, practical advice, and clear communication. His focus is to help clients understand their legal position and pursue the right remedy with confidence.

Whether you need guidance before taking legal action or representation in an ongoing civil matter, Adv. Pandey offers attentive, professional support from the first consultation through every stage of the case.
